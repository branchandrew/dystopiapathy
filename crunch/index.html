How the scores work

Every distinct thing a game makes you resolve counts as one crunch point: a roll, a table lookup, or a procedure with a decision in it, as written in the core book. Total is everything in the book; Minimum is the essential rules only, the game as most tables actually play it. A procedure that both sides of the table run counts once for the GM and once for the players, so GM + Player = Total. Individual spells, feats, powers and equipment don't count on their own: they scale past the core book. Popularity is an estimate.
